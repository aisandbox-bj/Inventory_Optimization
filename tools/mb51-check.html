MB51 stock check

Tests whether the way Trend reads MB51 rebuilds the stock on the Inventory Master, part by part, from SAP go-live. It runs Trend's rule next to the storage-location rule and the old movement list, and saves one results file to send back for review. It changes nothing in the app or your saved runs.

Your files are read in this browser only — nothing is uploaded or sent anywhere. The results file holds material numbers, quantities and, for parts that don't reconcile, their descriptions: treat it as client data.
